Topic Overview: World Wide Web, Internet vs Web, Static vs Dynamic Sites, HTML Structure[cite: 1].

1. World Wide Web (WWW)

Invented in 1989 by Sir Tim Berners-Lee at CERN. It is an information system where resources are identified by URLs and accessed over the Internet[cite: 1].

🗣️ Roman Urdu Explanation:
World Wide Web ko 1989 mein Sir Tim Berners-Lee ne CERN mein ijaad kiya tha[cite: 1]. Internet physical cables aur hardware ka jaal hai[cite: 1], jabke Web aik service hai jo internet ke upar chalti hai[cite: 1].
